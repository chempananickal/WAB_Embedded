\addchap{Confidentiality Notice}
\todo[inline]{
A confidentiality notice is only permitted if strictly \emph{information requiring protection}, confidential information is contained in the work. This is normally only assumed if internal company information of the employer \emph{without generalization, abstraction or anonymization is reproduced}.\\
The sources must be verifiable by the examiners despite the confidentiality notice. The persons assigned by the university for assessment and examination (directly and indirectly) must be authorized to use the contents of the examination performance.\\
The use of the (digital) documents for plagiarism detection purposes must be permitted.\\
Confidentiality notices that contradict this are either void or result in the work being considered as not submitted / the examination performance being rated as not performed.\\
In justified cases (see above), a confidentiality notice can look like this:}

\begin{verse}
    This work was prepared using confidential information from the company "XXX - please replace". This work may not be made accessible to third parties without express permission from the company "XXX - please replace".

    All uses for assessment by the university and service providers commissioned by it are explicitly permitted. Assessment also includes plagiarism checks and the use of artificial intelligence using appropriate IT platforms.
\end{verse}
