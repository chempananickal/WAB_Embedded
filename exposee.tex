\section*{1. Problem Statement and Motivation}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{1. Problem Statement and Motivation}

% Description of the problem statement and motivation for the work
The rapid growth of the \gls{IoT} has led to an increasing demand for \gls{LPWAN} technologies that can support a large number of connected devices over long distances. Two prominent \gls{LPWAN} technologies are \gls{LoRaWAN}, which operates in unlicensed spectrum, and \gls{NB-IoT}, which operates in licensed spectrum. This work aims to compare these two technologies in terms of their performance, advantages, and disadvantages in various application scenarios. The motivation for this comparison is to provide insights into which technology may be more suitable for specific use cases and to contribute to the ongoing discussion about the future of IoT connectivity.

\section*{3. Research Questions}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{3. Research Questions}

% Central research questions
\begin{itemize}
    \item What are the differences between \gls{LoRaWAN} and \gls{NB-IoT} in terms of range, data rate, power consumption, and cost?
    \item How do \gls{LoRaWAN} and \gls{NB-IoT} perform in real-world scenarios, such as urban and rural environments?
    \item What are the advantages and disadvantages of using unlicensed (\gls{LoRaWAN}) vs licensed (\gls{NB-IoT}) spectrum for \gls{LPWAN} technologies?
\end{itemize}

\section*{4. State of the Art}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{4. State of the Art}

% Overview of the current state of research
The state of the art in \gls{LPWAN} technologies includes various studies and comparisons of \gls{LoRaWAN} and \gls{NB-IoT}. \gls{LoRaWAN} is known for its long-range capabilities and low power consumption, making it suitable for applications such as smart agriculture and environmental monitoring. \gls{NB-IoT}, on the other hand, offers higher data rates and better reliability, which can be advantageous for applications like smart cities and industrial \gls{IoT}. However, there is still a need for comprehensive comparisons that consider real-world performance and the implications of using unlicensed vs licensed spectrum.
